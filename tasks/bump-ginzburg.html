Task 3: BG — Small representations, integral representations of L-functions and liftings

Study the covering-group integral and lifting constructions of Bump–Ginzburg (BG) and their collaborators: theta and small representations, Fourier coefficients, local and global zeta integrals, symmetric-power L-functions, orthogonal-cover liftings and cubic Shimura periods.

The principal works are the Bump–Ginzburg symmetric-square construction, the Bump–Ginzburg–Hoffstein symmetric-cube construction, and the work of Bump–Friedberg–Ginzburg on cubic Shimura lifting, small representations and liftings between orthogonal covers.

Mathematical checkpoints

  1. Theta and small representations. Construct the required local representations and global Eisenstein residues on general linear, orthogonal and exceptional covers. Prove irreducibility, Jacquet-module and constant-term formulas, square integrability and local/global compatibility in the relevant families.

  2. Fourier coefficients and orbit support. Define coefficients attached to unipotent subgroups and characters, locally through Jacquet modules and globally through integration. Prove the vanishing and nonvanishing statements identifying nilpotent-orbit support, distinguishing minimal representations from other small representations.

  3. Models and uniqueness. Construct the Whittaker, degenerate and other functionals needed by periods and integrals. Determine their dimensions and behavior under induction. Prove the uniqueness or nonunique-model arguments that give factorization, using the shared representation-theory definitions.

  4. Local zeta integrals. Define the relevant local functionals, prove convergence and compute unramified factors in terms of representation parameters. Establish required ramified and archimedean nonvanishing, normalization identities and compatibility with local intertwiners.

  5. Global integrals and L-functions. Define quotient measures and global integrals, prove convergence regions and justify unfolding. Derive Euler products and the established analytic consequences for symmetric-square, symmetric-cube and other covering-related integral constructions. Track which local model and nonvanishing theorem supports each factorization.

  6. Liftings on orthogonal covers. Construct kernels from small representations and the resulting operators between genuine automorphic representations of even and odd orthogonal double covers. Prove their nonvanishing, cuspidality, Fourier-coefficient and local compatibility properties, specifying the source and image.

  7. Cubic Shimura periods. Construct the period obtained by pulling the minimal representation of SO(8) back along the adjoint map from PGL(3). Prove the established identities and evidence relating this period to cubic Shimura lifting; state the image conjecture and the exact consequences of later advances separately.

The local and global integral theories use the common representation and measure conventions. Each checkpoint includes a worked application, readable proofs and checked Lean declarations; useful Fourier and model lemmas become shared library results.
