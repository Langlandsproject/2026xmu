Task 2: Kazhdan–Patterson — Representations, automorphic forms and metaplectic correspondence for GL(r)

Study the local representation theory and global automorphic theory of Kazhdan–Patterson covers of GL(r): covering tori, induction, exceptional representations, Whittaker models, Eisenstein series and metaplectic correspondence. Keep matrix size, covering degree and determinant twist as parameters; GL(2) is a worked example.

Treat Kazhdan–Patterson covers as a named family of Brylinski–Deligne covers in the library.

The foundational sources are Kazhdan and Patterson’s Metaplectic forms and their work towards a generalized Shimura correspondence, together with Flicker’s GL(2) theory and Flicker–Kazhdan metaplectic correspondence. The local programme also includes Zou’s correspondence results and the Kaplan–Lapid–Zou classification.

Main theorem targets

  1. Classification of genuine irreducible representations. Formalize the Kaplan–Lapid–Zou classification theorem: genuine irreducibles of the specified Kazhdan–Patterson covers are described by the appropriate multisegment data and compatible central character. Include the uniqueness, exhaustion and multiplicity-one assertions of the classification, with supercuspidal representations as its input data.

  2. Local metaplectic correspondence and its representation-theoretic consequences. Formalize the Flicker–Kazhdan character correspondence for the stated essentially square-integrable representations, using Zou’s corrected and extended results for Levi compatibility, parabolic induction and Jacquet modules. Include the proved reducibility criterion and the essentially square-integrable and tempered classification results in their precise ranges.

  3. Exceptional representations and metaplectic automorphic forms. Formalize the main local exceptional-representation results and global Eisenstein-residue theorems in Kazhdan–Patterson, using the published corrections. Target the proved representation-theoretic and Fourier-coefficient assertions, retaining the field, covering-degree and normalization hypotheses.

  4. Global metaplectic correspondence. Formalize the global correspondence results proved by Flicker and Flicker–Kazhdan, including the character and trace-formula identities used to establish them. Keep the stated automorphic-representation classes, central-character restrictions and multiplicity assertions attached to each theorem.

  5. Whittaker-dimension theorem: conditional formula and proved cases. Formalize the implication proved in Zou, §4, Theorem 4.7, retaining Conjecture 4.6 as an explicit hypothesis where it is used. Separately formalize the cases of that conjecture proved in §4.4, including the character and cuspidal cases specified there, and the resulting unconditional dimension statements.

Supporting material

The library definition of Kazhdan–Patterson covers, covering tori, metaplectic tensor products, induction, Jacquet modules, intertwiners and character theory support these theorems. Definitions and technical lemmas are introduced as required by their proofs. Archimedean or wild-place statements are included only when they occur in the cited theorem’s proved scope.
