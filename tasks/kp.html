Task 2: Kazhdan–Patterson — Representations, automorphic forms and metaplectic correspondence for GL(r)

Learn and formalize the local and global theory of Kazhdan–Patterson covers of GL(r), with irreducible-representation classification and metaplectic correspondence as the main goals. The foundational works are Kazhdan–Patterson on metaplectic forms, Kaplan–Lapid–Zou on classification, and Flicker and Flicker–Kazhdan on correspondence. For the correspondence, follow the character identities and trace-formula comparisons in those works.

Kazhdan–Patterson covers are treated directly as a named family of Brylinski–Deligne covers in the library. The formalization follows the main theorems and proofs of the core papers, retaining their central-character, field and covering-degree hypotheses.

Core references

  • Kazhdan-Patterson, Corrections to Metaplectic forms, IHES 62 (1985), 203. DOI.
  • Kaplan-Lapid-Zou, Classification of irreducible representations of metaplectic covers of the general linear group over a non-archimedean local field, Representation Theory 27 (2023), 1041-1087. DOI.
  • Flicker, Automorphic forms on covering groups of GL(2), Invent. Math. 57 (1980), 119-182. DOI.
  • Kazhdan-Patterson, Towards a generalized Shimura correspondence, Adv. Math. 60 (1986), 161-234. DOI.
  • Flicker-Kazhdan, Metaplectic correspondence, IHES 64 (1986), 53-110. DOI.
