AI-assisted research tasks

Four collaborative tasks to build a unified Lean library and a Lean-certified mathematical knowledge base for covering groups and their representations.

The work combines AI assistance with mathematical exposition, proof review and Lean formalization. The checkpoints describe work to be carried out; they are not a list of completed formalizations.

Building a Lean-certified knowledge base

Common requirements for precise statements, readable proofs, generality, consistent notation, reusable Lean interfaces, linked concepts and attribution.

Covering groups, BD invariants and L-groups

Central extensions and K2 torsors, BD classification, local and adelic realization, dual groups, L-groups, Iwahori-fixed vectors and Hecke theory.

Metaplectic representation theory of GL(r)

General-rank covers, induction, Jacquet modules, intertwiners, Whittaker spaces, representation classification, and local and global correspondence.

Small representations, periods, integrals and liftings

The covering-related Bump–Ginzburg programme: Fourier models, local and global integrals, L-functions, orthogonal-cover liftings and cubic Shimura periods.

Real representations, character lifting and unitarity

Real covering groups, pseudospherical correspondence, character lifting, multiplicity duality, small representations and unitarity, including specified complex-group families.

How the work fits together

The four teams contribute different parts of the same library. They share definitions of mathematical objects and reuse constructions and lemmas across the theory. The knowledge base follows those mathematical connections, with papers providing sources, historical context and proof methods.

The BD team develops covering-group foundations. The KP team develops general-linear representation theory. The Bump–Ginzburg team develops small representations, models and integral constructions. The real-group team supplies real representation theory, lifting and unitarity results.

The comparison between BD realizations and concrete KP covers is an additional task with its own checkpoint in both programmes. The KP main programme can proceed using its concrete covers through the common library interfaces.
