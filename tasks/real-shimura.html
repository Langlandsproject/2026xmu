Task 4: Adams–Vogan–Trapa — Character lifting and unitary Shimura correspondences for real covering groups

Study the work of Adams, Vogan, Trapa and their collaborators on Shimura correspondences, character lifting and unitarity for real covering groups, including nonsplit real forms and complex groups viewed as real Lie groups. The task covers both the foundational results and the later small-representation and lifting problems described below.

The main sources are Adams–Barbasch–Paul–Trapa–Vogan for unitary Shimura correspondence over split real groups; Adams–Herb for character lifting; Adams–Trapa for nonlinear multiplicity duality; and Adams–van Leeuwen–Trapa–Vogan for unitarity algorithms. The later lifting questions include work of Tsai and the Tsai–Wong–Zhang preprint listed in the references.

Mathematical checkpoints

  1. Representations and operators. Definitions of real covers, genuine characters, compact types, Harish-Chandra modules and the globalizations used in the references. Formalize their normalized induction, standard modules, intertwining operators and invariant Hermitian forms, together with the stated well-definedness and compatibility results.

  2. Pseudospherical and Howe correspondence. The associated linear group, parameter map, petite-type intertwiners and signature calculations in the cited works. Formalize the classical-group complementary-series injection and the symplectic unitarity equivalence for constituents containing det^(1/2). Record the broader bijection conjecture separately.

  3. Character lifting. For the admissible simply laced covering data specified in the sources, formalize the definitions of transfer factors and character lifting. Verify the theorem that stable virtual characters lift to genuine virtual characters, possibly zero, and the stated standard-module, induction and infinitesimal-character formulas.

  4. Blocks and multiplicity duality. Definitions of genuine Harish-Chandra blocks, standard and irreducible character expansions, and the Kazhdan–Lusztig–Vogan calculations used in the literature. Formalize the character-multiplicity duality theorem for admissible double covers of simply laced real groups at regular infinitesimal character, within its stated real-form hypotheses.

  5. Small representations. Formalize the literature’s calculation of the lift of the trivial representation, including its constituents, central characters and infinitesimal character rho/2. Verify the split simply laced theorem and the stated nonsplit type D results, where the lift may omit some small representations or vanish. Retain the preprint status of the 2026 nonsplit work.

  6. Unitarity beyond principal series. Formalize the literature’s Jantzen filtrations, signature changes at reducibility points and unitarity calculations. Verify the unitarity algorithm for linear real reductive groups and the nonlinear covering cases established in the cited works, keeping their precise scope explicit.

  7. Complex classical lifting. Study the Grothendieck-group lifts from SO(2r,C) to Spin(2r,C), and from Sp(2r,C) to Spin(2r+1,C). Verify the 2026 preprint's formulas for special unipotent sums and unitarity of their nonzero lifted constituents. This adds explicit families beyond the split-real pseudospherical setting.

Use common Cartan, parameter, character and Hermitian-form definitions throughout. Preprint results are targets for proof review.
