Task 3: Renard–Trapa and Adams–Barbasch–Paul–Trapa–Vogan — Vogan duality, Kazhdan–Lusztig–Vogan algorithms and unitary Shimura correspondences for real covering groups

Formalize David Renard and Peter Trapa’s Kazhdan–Lusztig–Vogan character algorithms and Vogan character-multiplicity duality for the real covering groups treated in their papers, including metaplectic groups and double covers of real general linear and unitary groups.

Formalize the unitary Shimura correspondence for split real groups developed by Adams, Barbasch, Paul, Trapa and Vogan through harmonic-analytic methods.

The proved results include the complementary-series injection for the stated classical groups and the unitarity equivalence under Howe correspondence for the metaplectic symplectic representations containing the det^(1/2) K-type. The broader correspondence is stated as a conjecture in that work.
