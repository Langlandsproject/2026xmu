Langlands Program for Covering Groups

When7–11 December 2026
Monday–Friday
WhereTianyuan Mathematical Center in Southeast China
Xiamen University · No. 1 Zengcuo'an West Road, Xiamen, China

Covering groups and the Langlands program

The conference brings together researchers working on the classical and geometric aspects of covering groups, creating a forum for lectures, research talks, and discussion.

Hosted by

Tianyuan Mathematical Center in Southeast China
Xiamen University
