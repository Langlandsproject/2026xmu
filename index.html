About the conference

The theory of covering groups has become an important branch of the Langlands program, with significant recent progress on both the classical (number-field) and the geometric (function-field) sides.

This conference brings together researchers from around the world to discuss the development of the theory of covering groups. It is hosted by the Tianyuan Mathematical Center in Southeast China at Xiamen University, one of the national mathematics centers established with the support of the National Natural Science Foundation of China.
